3D - Invertibility and Isomorphisms

Invertible Linear Maps 

======================================

linear map T: V->W 
           is invertible 

iff exists linear map S: W->V (inverse of T)

with ST = I (on V)
     TS = I (on W)

======================================

inverse of linear map T: V->W 
                      is unique 

S1 = (    I) S1 
   = (S2  T) S1 
   =  S2 (T  S1)
   =  S2 (    I) = S2 

======================================

linear map T:V->W 

T is invertible iff   T is  injective 
                    & T is surjective 

with dim V = dim W (finite dimensional)

======================================

Proof. Definition of properties (of T)

                         T        inverse 
invertible : ∀v (in V) ---> ∃w ---------> v (∴ injective)

                         inverse        T 
             ∀w (in W) ---------> ∃v ---> w (∴ surjective)

                  T 
 injective :  !v ---> ∀w (in range T)

                  T
surjective : ∃v ---> ∀w (in W)

======================================

Proof. (⇐)

(T is bijection; define S: W->V)

          T               S  
    ∃!v ---> ∀w (in W) ---> ∃!v

(TS = I on W) 

          T               S         T 
    ∃!v ---> ∀w (in W) ---> ∃!v ---> w

(ST = I on V)

    consider TS = I (on W)

                T      S         T 
    ∀v (in V) ---> w ---> (??) ---> w 

                                 T 
                             !v ---> w 

(S is linear: additivity)

             S                 T 
    w1 + w2 ---> S( w1 +  w2) ---> w1 + w2 

                               T 
                 !(Sw1 + Sw2) ---> w1 + w2

(S is linear: homogeneity)

        S          T 
    aw ---> S(aw) ---> aw 

                   T 
           !(aSw) ---> aw 

======================================

dim V = dim W (finite dimensional)

linear map T: V->W 

then T invertible iff T injective 
                  iff T surjective 

because

dim V = dim null T  + dim range T --- Fundamental Theorem

  n   =     0       +      n
        (injective)   (surjective)

either alone gives bijection, hence invertible
