Linear Map Lemma 

v(1) ... v(n) : basis of V
w(1) ... w(n) : in W

then 
exists unique linear map T: V->W

      T
v(k) ---> w(k)

===================================================

Proof of Linear Map Lemma : Uniqueness

suppose linear map T : V -> W 

                 T 
            v_k --->             w_k  
       (c_k)v_k --->        (c_k)w_k : by Homogeneity
sum of (c_k)v_k ---> sum of (c_k)w_k : by Additivity

===================================================

Proof of Linear Map Lemma : Exstence

                 T
sum of (c_k)v_k ---> sum of (c_k)w_k

(1) well-defined : v(k) has unique w(k)

(2) Tv(k) = w(k) : c(k) = 1 and other c's = 0

(3) Additivity

                             T 
  u =    sum of [ a_k ] v_k --->   sum of [ a_k ] w_k  =   Tu 
+ v    + sum of [ c_k ] v_k      + sum of [ c_k ] w_k    + Tv 

(4) Homogeneity

                          T 
    sum of (a * c_k) v_k --->     sum of (a * c_k) w_k 
= a sum of (c_k) v_k          = a sum of     (c_k) w_k
= a v                         = a T(v)
