Invertible Linear Maps 

======================================

linear map T: V->W 
           is invertible 

iff exists linear map S: W->V (inverse of T)

with ST = I (on V)
     TS = I (on W)

======================================

inverse of linear map T: V->W 
                      is unique 

S1 = (    I) S1 
   = (S2  T) S1 
   =  S2 (T  S1)
   =  S2 (    I) = S2 

======================================

linear map T:V->W 

T is invertible iff   T is  injective 
                    & T is surjective 

with dim V = dim W (finite dimensional)

T is invertible iff T injective 
                iff T surjective 

(∵ Fundamental Theorem)

======================================

Proof. Definition of properties (of T)

                         T        inverse 
invertible : ∀v (in V) ---> ∃w ---------> v (∴ injective)

                         inverse        T 
             ∀w (in W) ---------> ∃v ---> w (∴ surjective)

                  T 
 injective :  !v ---> ∀w (in range T)

                  T
surjective : ∃v ---> ∀w (in W)
