3D - Invertibility and Isomorphisms

Invertible linear maps 

======================================

linear map T : V->W is invertible 

iff exists linear map S : W->V (inverse of T)

with ST = I : V->V ----- injective 
     TS = I : W->W ----- surjective

======================================

linear map T : V->W

T^(-1) := inverse of T is unique 

   S1          I 
v ----> w -----------> w  

   S1      T      S2  
v ----> w ---> v ----> w 

       I          S2  
v -----------> v ----> w 

======================================

linear map T : V->W is invertible 

iff T is injective & surjective

======================================

Proof. 

(has inverse)  

                  T      T^(-1)
 injectivity : v ---> w --------> !v 

                    T^(-1)        T
surjectivity : ∀w --------> ∃v ---> w

======================================

(T is bijection)

          T
    ∃!v ---> ∀w 

(define S : W->V)

          T        S         T
    ∃!v ---> ∀w ---> ∃!v ---> w 

                        I   
              ∀w -------------> w 

    therefore TS = I (on W) by definition of S 

(ST = I: V->V)

    consider TS = I (on W)

         T      S         T 
    ∀v ---> w ---> (??) ---> w   

                          T 
                      !v ---> w

    therefore ST = I (on V) by injectivity of T

(S is linear : additivity)

               T
  Sw1 + Sw2  ---> TSw1 + TSw2 = w1 + w2 --- additivity of T ; TS = I
  S(w1 + w2) --->               w1 + w2 --- TS = I

; S(w1 + w2) = Sw1 + Sw2 --- injectivity of T

(S is linear : homogeneity)

          T
  a(Sw)  ---> a(TSw) = aw --- homogeneity of T ; TS = I
  S(aw)  --->          aw --- TS = I

; S(aw) = a(Sw) --- injectivity of T
